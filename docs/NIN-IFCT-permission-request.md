Respected Sir/Madam,

I am writing to request written permission to use the food-composition data published in the **Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT) 2017** (Longvah, T., Ananthan, R., Bhaskarachary, K. and Venkaiah, K., 2017; National Institute of Nutrition, Indian Council of Medical Research, Hyderabad).

I note from the copyright page of IFCT 2017 that while the use and dissemination of the data is encouraged, and reproduction for personal use is permitted with full acknowledgment, "no part of this publication can be stored or reproduced in any electronic format for creating a product without the prior written permission of the National Institute of Nutrition." I am therefore respectfully seeking that written permission.

**About the project**
- **Name:** Rituva — a nutrition and meal-planning application.
- **Nature:** free and **open-source (non-commercial)**. Source code: https://github.com/ankitjha67/Rituva
- **Purpose:** it generates guideline-grounded, seasonal, non-repeating meal plans for individuals and households, grounded in ICMR-NIN's *Dietary Guidelines for Indians (DGI 2024)*. It uses IFCT 2017 as the nutrient reference so that every nutrition value shown to a user is **authentic and computed from published data, never machine-generated or invented**.
- **Intended use of IFCT data:** to store the per-100 g nutrient values (energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, dietary fibre, minerals, etc.) in the application's internal food database, used solely to compute the nutrient content of meals for the user. Every value would carry an explicit **"Source: IFCT 2017"** citation.

**My requests**
1. Whether NIN grants permission to include IFCT 2017 data in this application, and under what conditions.
2. The exact citation / acknowledgment wording you would like displayed.
3. Any restrictions you wish to impose (e.g., non-commercial only, no redistribution of the raw table, attribution placement).

I am happy to provide any further information, sign an undertaking, or adjust the implementation to meet NIN's requirements. I will prominently acknowledge **IFCT 2017 and ICMR-NIN** as the data source within the application and its documentation.

Thank you for producing this invaluable resource and for considering my request.
